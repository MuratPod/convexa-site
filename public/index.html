How it’s calculated

Every leg repriced at every move

1. Positions

Legs, quantities, and prices come from your IBKR book. Convexa reads your book and never places trades.

2. Reprice

For each QQQ move, every option is repriced with a standard option model and summed into one P&L curve.

3. Two vol views

Constant vol (blue on the smile) keeps today’s implied vol. Realized vol (gold) lets vol follow the move.
