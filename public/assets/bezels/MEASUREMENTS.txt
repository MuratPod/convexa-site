Apple Product Bezel PNG measurements
Measured 2026-11-20 by scanning the alpha channel with Pillow + OpenCV.
Screen rect is the bounding box of the largest fully transparent (alpha == 0)
connected component that does not touch the image border — i.e. the screen hole.
Coordinates are (x, y, w, h) in pixels, origin at the top-left of the PNG.
Corner radius is estimated from how far the hole is inset at each corner and
how many rows/columns it takes to reach the straight edge. A 50% alpha contour
gives a slightly smaller radius because of antialiasing; compositing should use
the alpha==0 rect and put the bezel PNG on top of the screenshot.
