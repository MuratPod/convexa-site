Apple Design Resources — Product Bezels
License and usage notes
=======================================

KEY RULES (summary)
-------------------
1. Use the official bezels “as is” and without modification: do not crop, tilt, flip, spin, animate, stretch, recolor, or distort the hardware; do not add reflections, shadows, highlights, cases/covers, or any graphic that appears to enter or come out of the screen.
2. Do not put promotional copy, badges, violators, or other overlays on the bezel itself. Copy may sit beside the product image, not on top of it. The only content that belongs in the transparent screen cutout is a realistic image of the app running (including lock-screen Live Activities when that is what you are showing).
3. Use straight-on shots of the supplied device colors; keep products at correct relative scale; on-screen height must be at least 200 px (25 mm in print). Always use the latest-generation devices for which the app is developed, when that is what the guidelines require.
4. The license shipped in each pack is a limited, royalty-free license to use these product images only to market an App Store application, and only while the licensee is an Apple Developer Program member. Apple remains the exclusive owner of the artwork.
5. These terms can change. Re-read the Marketing Resources and Identity Guidelines before publishing: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/#section-products
   Also: https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html

Relevant excerpt from the Marketing Resources and Identity Guidelines
(Product Images / Graphic Standards / Approved Devices, retrieved 2026-11-20):

  “Use Apple-provided product bezels in all your marketing materials to display
  your app on the Apple devices it supports. Always use the latest-generation
  devices for which your app is currently developed.”

  “Use Apple product images “as is” and without modification. Modifications
  include adding reflections, shadows, highlights, or graphic elements that
  appear to enter or come out of the product screen; cropping, tilting, or
  obstructing any part of the images; animating, flipping, or spinning the
  images; or creating buttons or icons with a product image. You can place
  promotional copy or violators beside the product images, not on top of them.”

  “Use Apple product images at a size that is clearly legible at the image
  resolution provided. Ensure that the minimum device size is no smaller than
  25 mm in height for printed materials and 200 px onscreen. Maintain the
  correct relative product scale whenever multiple products are shown.”

  “Straight-on product shots are preferred. Don’t use extreme angles or alter
  an Apple product in any way. The product’s color, shape, size, and form
  must be accurate.”

  “Use only the devices and colors provided. If multiple Apple products are
  shown, display them in the correct relative sizes. Always display the
  product “as is” — don’t add cases or covers.”

  “When showing push notifications from your app in advertising, display a
  single push notification on the lock screen of the Apple device.”
